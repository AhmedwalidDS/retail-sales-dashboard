## 📌 Overview

> End-to-end retail sales analysis covering **1,000+ transactions** across 5 regions and 5 product categories.  
> The project uncovers revenue trends, top-performing products, regional breakdowns, and profit margin insights — all visualized through clean, publication-ready charts.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze monthly revenue and profit trends
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Compare regional sales performance
- Build a profit margin heatmap (Region × Category)
- Export a clean, analysis-ready dataset

---

## 📊 Key Visualizations

| Chart | Insight |
|-------|---------|
| 📈 Monthly Revenue Trend | Seasonal peaks in Q2 & Q4 |
| 🏷️ Revenue by Category | Electronics leads all categories |
| 🗺️ Regional Performance | North & West outperform other regions |
| 🏆 Top 10 Products | Laptop & Bicycle drive highest revenue |
| 🔥 Profit Margin Heatmap | Food & Beverages has the highest margins |

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- 💰 **Electronics** is the #1 revenue-generating category across all regions
- 🌍 **North** region consistently leads in both revenue and profit
- 📅 Revenue peaks occur in **Q2 and Q4** — ideal for promotional campaigns
- 🍫 **Food & Beverages** achieves the highest profit margins despite lower prices
